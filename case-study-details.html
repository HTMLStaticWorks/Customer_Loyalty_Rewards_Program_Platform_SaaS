How North & Co. Unified Omnichannel Loyalty & Boosted Repeat Rates by 56%

A deep dive into how a premium apparel retailer connected 4 physical boutiques and their DTC storefront with native Apple & Google Wallet passes.

Industry Apparel & Fashion Retail
Locations 4 Stores + DTC Web
Active Members 124,000+

The Challenge

Prior to implementing LOYALLOOP, North & Co. struggled with fragmented customer identification. Over 65% of their in-store shoppers never created an online account, and online DTC buyers rarely mentioned their purchase history when visiting the physical boutique locations.

Their existing paper loyalty card had an abysmal 14% redemption rate, cashiers found manual email lookups too slow during busy Saturday afternoons, and one-time seasonal shoppers were rarely converted into repeat buyers.

The LOYALLOOP Strategy

North & Co. partnered with LOYALLOOP to deploy a frictionless digital pass infrastructure:

  • Instant 1-Tap Wallet Pass: Placed QR codes at boutique fitting rooms and cash registers allowing shoppers to save their membership card to Apple or Google Wallet in 5 seconds.
  • Unified Tier System: Created Member, Silver, and Gold VIP tiers where members earn 10 pts per $1, unlocking early seasonal product launches.
  • Geofence Foot Traffic Prompts: Triggered lock-screen balance notifications whenever members walked within 200m of any North & Co. flagship storefront.
