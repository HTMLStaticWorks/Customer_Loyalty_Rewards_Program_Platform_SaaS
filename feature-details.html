Dynamic Points & Multi-Tier Rewards Engine

Motivate repeat purchases with flexible point accrual rules, tiered multiplier perks, and native smartphone wallet passes that update automatically with every swipe.

The Legacy Dilemma

Traditional plastic loyalty punch cards get forgotten in wallets, lost at home, or tossed away. Paper stamps fail to capture customer emails or purchase habits, and generic 10% discount codes destroy profit margins without building genuine affinity.

  • 82% of paper cards are discarded within 30 days
  • No mechanism to re-engage lapsed customers
  • Zero omnichannel continuity between in-store and online shopping

The LOYALLOOP Solution

A digital-first, wallet-native loyalty engine that lives permanently in the customer's Apple or Google Wallet. Points accrue in real-time, milestone badges unlock visually, and lock-screen push alerts drive timely repeat visits.

  • 89%+ retention rate on mobile wallet passes
  • Automated re-engagement notifications when near your stores
  • Full synchronization across POS terminals and ecommerce carts
Architectural Flow

How the Engine Operates Behind the Scenes

Built on a secure, low-latency rules engine that processes member transactions in milliseconds.

01

Transaction Ingestion

When an order occurs at POS or online checkout, LOYALLOOP ingests the basket total, product SKU categories, and member identifier.

02

Rules & Multipliers Evaluated

The rules engine applies active tier multipliers (e.g. 1.5x for Gold), birthday bonuses, and promotional day rules to compute the final points earned.

03

Real-Time Wallet Push

The member's digital pass is instantly refreshed with the new balance, triggering a haptic celebration notification on their smartphone.
