Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers regarding LOYALLOOP setup, POS integration, wallet passes, and member data privacy.

General & Architecture

LOYALLOOP is a modern customer engagement SaaS platform that eliminates the need for plastic loyalty cards and cumbersome mobile app downloads. Instead, it utilizes native Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes that live directly inside the customer's smartphone, updating point balances and sending lock-screen geofence notifications in real-time.

Over 70% of shoppers abandon loyalty programs when forced to download an app. With LOYALLOOP, customers simply tap a link on their smartphone receipt or scan a QR code at your register to save their pass directly to their phone's built-in digital wallet.

Programs & Rewards

Yes. You can configure multiple reward tracks. For instance, a café can run a 10-visit coffee stamp pass for daily espresso regulars while running a spend-based point system for catering and merchandise purchases.

Tier upgrades occur instantaneously. As soon as a customer crosses a defined spend or visit milestone, their digital pass transforms dynamically on their screen with the new tier artwork (e.g., Gold VIP) and a celebratory push notification is triggered.

Implementation & POS Setup

Any standard 2D barcode scanner (Honeywell, Zebra, etc.) connected to your POS terminal can read the dynamic QR or PDF417 barcodes on LOYALLOOP passes. Alternatively, store associates can use our mobile web scanner on any tablet or smartphone.

Most single-location merchants configure and launch their program in less than 15 minutes using our visual pass builder and pre-configured reward templates.
