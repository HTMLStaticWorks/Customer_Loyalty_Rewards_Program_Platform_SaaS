Core Infrastructure

01. Dynamic Loyalty Engine

Configure flexible earning mechanics tailored to your business model—from spend-based cashback to frequency stamps and game mechanics.

  • Spend-Based Accrual: Award 1–10 points per dollar with multiplier rules for specific categories or promo days.
  • Visit-Based Stamps: Perfect for coffee shops, bakeries, and casual dining to reward frequent visits.
  • Apple & Google Wallet Native: Dynamic lock-screen passes that update automatically after every scan.

Points Programs

Custom point valuations, expiration policies, and redemption thresholds.

Visit-Based Rewards

Punch-card style digital passes with progressive milestone rewards.

Spend-Based Rewards

Tiered cashback percentages automatically applied at POS checkout.

Digital Pass Generator

Drag-and-drop designer for personalized smartphone wallet passes.

Voucher & Coupon Engine

Generate single-use, timed, or minimum-spend promo discount codes.

Birthday Celebrations

Automated festive gifts that bring shoppers into your store during their birthday month.

Milestone Unlocks

Celebrate 5th visit, 10th visit, or $500 lifetime spend with exclusive merchandise.

Free Product Catalog

Allow members to swap accumulated points for high-margin physical products.

Catalog Flexibility

02. Flexible Reward Catalog

Reward your community with tangible perks that protect your margins while maximizing emotional loyalty.

Choose between instant dollar discounts, free physical merchandise, experiential privileges, or digital vouchers. Update your catalog dynamically without re-issuing member passes.

Smart Triggers

03. Automated Engagement Campaigns

Trigger the right message at the right moment across push notifications, SMS, and email.

  • Geofence Proximity Alerts: Lock-screen notifications pop up when members are within 200m of your location.
  • At-Risk Churn Win-Back: Automatically detect when a regular hasn't visited in 45 days and issue a "We Miss You" incentive.
  • RFM Segmentation: Group members into Champions, Loyalists, At-Risk, and Hibernating tiers automatically.
Aspirational Status

04. Multi-Tier Memberships

Structure Bronze, Silver, Gold, and VIP tiers. Reward customers as they spend more with accelerated point earnings and exclusive perks.

  • Tier status maintenance rules
  • Tier upgrade celebratory rewards
  • Custom tier badge designs on digital passes
